The Author

Amal Iman Ameen

Writing as Esper Ayla.

Amal Iman Ameen is a Sri Lankan author and novel artist writing under the pen name Esper Ayla. Her stories move between contemporary romance, psychological depth, emotion, and the beautiful darkness found between them. She doesn't simply want to put stories on a page. She wants to see them before they exist — the atmosphere, the expressions, the locations, the silence, the movement. Somewhere between the page and the screen, her stories begin.
