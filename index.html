Mavis

A novel by Amal Iman Ameen

Some people leave. Some people stay. And some stories refuse to let you go.

Set during the restless years of college life, Mavis follows Amalyn — a guarded, fiercely independent young woman who has long stopped believing in fairy tales.

She knows how to protect herself.
What she doesn't know is how to let people stay.

Then come Zayn and Ryan — two very different presences who find their way into the world Amalyn has carefully built around herself.

One brings tenderness.
One brings a storm.

And somewhere between friendship, vulnerability, heartbreak and a love she never expected to feel, Amalyn begins to discover that keeping people at a distance doesn't necessarily protect you from losing them.

Somewhere along the way, the lines between what she fears, what she wants, and what she is willing to feel begin to disappear.

And when love begins asking questions she isn't ready to answer—

Amalyn has to face the one thing she has spent years trying not to become: vulnerable.

The Author
“I don't just write stories. I want you to see them.”

Amal Iman Ameen is an author and novel artist writing under the pen name Esper Ayla. Drawn to literature from childhood, she experiences stories visually, often imagining them as films without screens. Through her writing, she seeks to create worlds that readers can see, feel and experience.
