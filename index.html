Writing as Esper Ayla.

Amal Iman Ameen is a Sri Lankan author and novel artist writing under the pen name Esper Ayla.

She doesn't simply write stories. She sees them — imagining atmosphere, expressions, locations, movement and emotion as though the page were already a screen.

Somewhere between the page and the screen, her stories begin.
