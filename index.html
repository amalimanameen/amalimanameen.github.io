The Author
“I don't just write stories. I want you to see them.”

Amal Iman Ameen is an author and novel artist writing under the pen name Esper Ayla. Drawn to literature from childhood, she experiences stories visually, often imagining them as films without screens. Through her writing, she seeks to create worlds that readers can see, feel and experience.
