01 · The Beginning

Writing was never just writing.

Amal Iman Ameen has loved authors and stories since childhood. Literature was never simply something to study. It was something to enter.

From Oscar Wilde's The Nightingale and the Rose, to Heidi, Oliver Twist, and Shakespeare's Romeo and Juliet, she became fascinated not only by the stories themselves, but by the minds that created them.

She would visualize what she read as though a film were playing without a screen — the atmosphere, expressions, clothing, locations, movement, silence, even the breeze.

02 · The Craft

The Novel Artist.

Amal does not want to simply write scenes. She wants to see them.

To construct them. Stage them. Feel the characters. Imagine the camera, the atmosphere, the expressions, the locations — even the smallest movement of the air.

Her goal is to direct through words while allowing the reader to experience something beyond what words alone appear capable of doing.

If she feels something while writing, she hopes her readers will feel it twice as deeply while reading.

Somewhere between the page and the screen, her stories begin.
03 · The Name

Esper Ayla.

Amal writes under the name Esper Ayla, a name connected to hope and light within darkness.

Hope has always carried a personal meaning for her. Her own name, Amal, means hope — and writing became connected to the hope someone once placed in her, as well as the hope she carries for the childhood dream she never abandoned.
