BACKGROUND PROCESS // SECOND PRESENCE DETECTED

Iris Vale has spent years living beside a silence she cannot solve. Her grandmother, Dr. Elara Vale, has been in a coma for six years. The questions she left behind have never stopped multiplying.

When Iris is pulled toward an experimental neural architecture, the boundary between interface and consciousness begins to fracture. Somewhere inside the system is K-17 — Kaelen — and the deeper Iris goes, the less certain she becomes that she is the one doing the searching.

This is not a story about a machine learning to think. It is about what happens when obsession finds an architecture capable of remembering it.
