The Story

She never believed in fairy tales.

Amalyn knows how to survive on her own. She knows how to keep people at a distance, how to hide what hurts, and how to leave before anyone gets the chance to leave her.

Then Ryan arrives.

Mysterious. Playful. Persistent.

He doesn't ask permission to challenge the walls around her — he simply keeps finding his way through them.

And somewhere between arguments, laughter, vulnerability and the kind of love neither of them expected, Amalyn begins to wonder if perhaps she was wrong about letting someone in.

Until fate asks a question neither of them is prepared to answer.

What happens when the person who teaches you how to love becomes the person fate never intended you to keep?

The Characters

The hearts within Mavis.

Amalyn
The guarded heart

Independent, guarded and carrying wounds she rarely allows anyone to see. Amalyn doesn't believe in fairy tales, but beneath her defenses lies someone still trying to understand how to let people close without losing them.

Zayn
The tender heart

Tender and playful, Zayn carries a warmth that reaches places Amalyn would rather keep hidden. He becomes one of the people capable of making her defenses tremble.

Ryan
The unexpected storm

Mysterious, playful and persistent, Ryan enters Amalyn's life without asking whether she is ready. He pushes against the edges of everything she thought she knew about herself.

The Reader's Journey

What Mavis is meant to make you feel.

Vulnerability

The slow and painful dismantling of the defenses people build to protect themselves.

First Love

The aching intensity of something raw, imperfect and impossible to experience halfway.

Longing

The quiet anticipation that something beautiful may be changed by forces neither heart can control.

From the Author

Writing is Love.

Mavis began as something deeply personal — a lament of the heart transformed into fiction. Amal hopes that what she felt while writing these pages becomes something her readers can feel in their own way. Not simply something they read. Something they see. Something they carry.
